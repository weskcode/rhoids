# iPhone screenshots

The ten English screenshots in `en-US/` are the approved App Store Connect set for RHOIDS 1.1.2. Each image is 1320 × 2868 pixels, opaque RGB, with an sRGB profile. Filenames specify the display order; `manifest.json` records their SHA-256 checksums.

The images combine existing app captures with captions and upright device frames. The Watch clock is normalized to 9:41; countdown values are unchanged. All ten assets finished processing in App Store Connect, where their checksums and order were verified.

These marketing assets do not establish current-build or physical-device verification. Raw captures, rendering tools and upload logs are kept outside the public repository.
