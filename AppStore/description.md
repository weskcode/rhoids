## Description

RHOIDS is a privacy-first bathroom timer that helps you stop scrolling on the toilet and keep bathroom breaks short.

Long toilet sessions are one of the easiest habits to ignore. They are also one of the simplest to change. RHOIDS gives you a clear countdown, a hard stopping point, and persistent reminders across iPhone and Apple Watch so you know when it is time to get up.

Built around published research and clinical guidance, RHOIDS keeps the experience focused: no wellness dashboard, no social feed, no account setup, no ads, and no tracking. Just a fast toilet timer designed for one specific job.

WHY USE A TOILET TIMER?

A 2025 Harvard Medical School and Beth Israel Deaconess study published in PLOS One linked smartphone use on the toilet with a 46% higher risk of hemorrhoids. The same study found that 37% of phone users spent more than five minutes per bathroom visit.

RHOIDS turns that research into a simple daily habit: start the timer, finish your business, and leave.

WHAT YOU GET

- Recommended timer: a 3-minute preset for quick bathroom breaks
- Max timer: a 5-minute limit based on common medical guidance
- Custom timer: choose your own duration when you need flexibility
- Live Activity: see time remaining on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
- Widgets: start a bathroom timer from the Home Screen or Lock Screen
- Apple Watch: follow the countdown with haptics and complications
- Siri Shortcuts: start and stop timers hands-free
- Focus Lock: block selected distracting apps after the timer ends
- Local history: review recent sessions without sending data anywhere

BUILT FOR PRIVACY

RHOIDS is designed for a private habit, so it stays private.

- No accounts
- No tracking
- No ads
- No social features
- No third-party dependencies
- Session history stays on your device

SCIENCE WITHOUT THE CLUTTER

RHOIDS includes an in-app Science section with the research behind the timers, including the 2025 PLOS One smartphone-use study, broader hemorrhoid prevalence research, and guidance from major medical sources. The goal is not to scare you or turn bathroom breaks into a fitness metric. The goal is to make one healthy behavior easier to repeat.

RHOIDS is a behavior timer, not a medical device or medical treatment. If you have bleeding, pain, persistent symptoms, or medical concerns, talk with a qualified healthcare professional.

Download RHOIDS and build the simplest bathroom habit: start the timer, stop scrolling, and get off the toilet on time.
