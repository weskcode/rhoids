# RHOIDS Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

RHOIDS is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

## Data Stored on Your Devices

RHOIDS stores timer settings, preferences, streak information, and session history locally on your devices. Shared App Group storage allows the iPhone app, widgets, Live Activities, Screen Time extensions, and Apple Watch features to coordinate timer state.

## Apple System Services

RHOIDS may use Apple-provided services when you enable or invoke related features:

- Notifications and AlarmKit for timer warnings and completion alerts
- ActivityKit and WidgetKit for Live Activities and widgets
- Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings for Focus Lock
- WatchConnectivity for communication between iPhone and Apple Watch
- StoreKit for optional tip-jar purchases

These services are governed by Apple’s platform and privacy terms. Purchase processing is handled by Apple; RHOIDS does not receive your payment-card information.

## Data Collection and Sharing

RHOIDS does not operate a server that collects app usage, timer history, selected apps, identifiers, location, health information, or advertising data. RHOIDS does not sell or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

If you contact support, the information you choose to include in your message is used only to respond to that request.

## Your Choices

You can disable notifications, AlarmKit, Focus Lock, or other permissions in system Settings. Session history and app data can be removed using the app’s controls or by deleting the app, subject to normal Apple platform behavior.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when app behavior or platform requirements change. The revision date above identifies the current version.
